# Contract Constitution

**Status:** Draft (Sprint 1)
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Purpose:** The law governing the Contract Aggregate. Every implementation decision must comply with this document.

---

## Preamble

A Contract is the central business object of Muraai. It represents a legally binding software maintenance agreement between the enterprise (Licensee) and a Vendor. It is the source of truth for what was purchased, under what terms, and for how long. Everything else in Muraai — Renewals, Purchase Orders, Invoices — exists in service of the Contract.

This constitution defines the immutable laws that govern Contract identity, lifecycle, ownership, and invariants. No code, no feature, no integration may violate these laws.

---

## Article I: Identity

### §1.1 Definition

A Contract is a legally binding agreement between exactly one Licensee (the Muraai enterprise) and exactly one Vendor for a defined set of Products over a defined term.

### §1.2 Immutability

A Contract, once Activated, is immutable with respect to:
- **Vendor** — The vendor cannot change after activation. A change of vendor requires a new Contract.
- **Identity** — `contract_id` is assigned at creation and never changes.
- **Creation timestamp** — `created_at` is set once and never updated.

### §1.3 Historical Truth

A Contract is the historical source of truth for:
- What products were covered and at what rates (via ContractLineItem)
- What terms were agreed (via ContractClause)
- What documents support the agreement (via ContractDocument)
- What renewals occurred over its lifetime (via Renewal references)

### §1.4 Contract Number

Every Contract has a unique `contract_number`. Format: `MCR-{YEAR}-{SEQ:05d}`. Generated by the system. Human-readable. Unique across all time (even soft-deleted contracts reserve their number).

## Article II: Ownership

### §2.1 Mandatory References

| Reference | Cardinality | Nullable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vendor | Exactly 1 | No | The organization providing the software/service |
| Products | 1 or more | No | At least one product line item required for Activation |
| Documents | 0 or more | Yes | Supporting agreements, SOWs, amendments |
| Clauses | 0 or more | Yes | Legal terms and conditions |
| Renewals | 0 or more | Yes | Renewal cycles over the contract's lifetime |
| Activities | 0 or more | Yes | Activity log entries referencing this contract |
| User (owner) | Exactly 1 | No | The internal user responsible for this contract |

### §2.2 Aggregate Boundaries

The Contract Aggregate owns:
- `ContractLineItem` — products covered under the contract (composition)
- `ContractDocument` — documents attached to the contract (composition)
- `ContractClause` — legal terms (composition)
- `ContractVersionHistory` — version snapshots (composition)

The Contract Aggregate references (does not own):
- `Vendor` — via vendor_id FK
- `User` — via created_by, last_modified_by, user_id FKs
- `Renewal` — via contract_id FK on Renewal (Renewal is a separate aggregate)
- `PurchaseOrder` — via contract_id FK on PO
- `Invoice` — via contract_id FK on Invoice

### §2.3 Child Entity Rules

- A `ContractLineItem` cannot exist without a parent Contract. Cascade soft-delete.
- A `ContractDocument` cannot exist without a parent Contract. Cascade soft-delete.
- A `ContractClause` cannot exist without a parent Contract. Cascade soft-delete.
- A `ContractVersionHistory` entry is auto-generated on every state change. Immutable.

## Article III: Invariants

### §3.1 Structural Invariants

| ID | Invariant | Enforcement | Violation Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR-C-001 | `end_date` must be after `start_date` | DB CHECK constraint | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-C-002 | `total_contract_value` must equal sum of all line item `line_total` values | Service layer on save | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-C-003 | `renewal_date` must be on or before `end_date` if set | Validation | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-C-004 | `renewal_window_days` must be between 30 and 365 | Validation | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-C-005 | At least one ContractLineItem is required when status transitions to ACTIVE | Service layer | Reject with 409 |
| CTR-C-006 | `contract_number` must be unique | DB UNIQUE constraint | Reject with 409 |
| CTR-C-007 | `vendor_id` must reference a valid Vendor | DB FK constraint | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-C-008 | `notification_email` is required when `notification_enabled = true` | Validation | Reject with 422 |

### §3.2 Lifecycle Invariants

| ID | Invariant | Enforcement | Violation Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR-L-001 | A Contract cannot transition from DRAFT to ACTIVE without at least one line item | Service layer | Reject with 409 |
| CTR-L-002 | A Contract cannot transition from ACTIVE to DRAFT | State machine | Reject with 409 |
| CTR-L-003 | A Contract cannot transition to EXPIRED by user action — only by end_date | Automatic (scheduler) | Not user-triggerable |
| CTR-L-004 | An EXPIRED contract cannot be directly set to ACTIVE — must go through renewal | State machine | Reject with 409 |
| CTR-L-005 | A TERMINATED contract cannot be reactivated without admin override | State machine + role check | Reject with 403 |
| CTR-L-006 | A Contract with active Renewals cannot be TERMINATED | Service layer | Reject with 409 |
| CTR-L-007 | An ACTIVE contract cannot change its vendor | Immutable field rule | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-L-008 | Only DRAFT contracts can be hard-deleted. All other statuses soft-delete. | Service layer | Reject with 409 |

### §3.3 Business Invariants

| ID | Invariant | Enforcement | Violation Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR-B-001 | `total_contract_value` must be ≥ 0 | DB CHECK + validation | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-B-002 | Line item `quantity` must be ≥ 1 | Validation | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-B-003 | Line item `unit_rate` must be ≥ 0 | Validation | Reject with 422 |
| CTR-B-004 | Line item `discount_percent` must be 0-100 | Validation | Reject with 422 |

## Article IV: Lifecycle

### §4.1 States

```
DRAFT ────► ACTIVE ────► EXPIRED
               │
               ├──────────► TERMINATED
               │
               ├──────────► INCONSISTENT ──► ACTIVE (corrected)
               │
               └──────────► ARCHIVED (after retention period)

DRAFT ────► DELETED (only from DRAFT)
```

### §4.2 State Descriptions

| State | Category | Meaning | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| DRAFT | Initial | Being created. Not governing any renewals. | Minutes to days |
| ACTIVE | Active | In effect. Renewal monitoring active. | 1-5 years |
| EXPIRED | Terminal | End date passed without renewal. | Permanent |
| TERMINATED | Terminal | Ended early by user action. | Permanent |
| INCONSISTENT | Error | Data integrity issue detected. | Hours to days |
| ARCHIVED | Terminal | Retained for compliance, no longer operational. | Permanent |
| DELETED | Terminal | Permanently removed (DRAFT only). | Permanent |

### §4.3 Transitions

| From | To | Trigger | Automation | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DRAFT | ACTIVE | User activates | Manual | ≥1 line item, all mandatory fields, vendor valid |
| DRAFT | DELETED | User deletes | Manual | No renewals linked |
| ACTIVE | EXPIRED | end_date passed + no active renewal | Scheduled | `end_date < today` AND no Renewal in active states |
| ACTIVE | TERMINATED | User terminates | Manual | Reason required, no active Renewals |
| ACTIVE | INCONSISTENT | Integrity check fails | Scheduled (daily) or post-update | Total ≠ sum(line_items), end_date < start_date, etc. |
| ACTIVE | ARCHIVED | Retention period passed | Scheduled | `end_date + retention_period < today` |
| ACTIVE | ACTIVE | User edits permitted fields | Manual | No changes to immutable fields |
| EXPIRED | ACTIVE | Renewal processed | Automatic | Via Renewal aggregate (renewal.completed event) |
| TERMINATED | ACTIVE | Admin reversal | Manual | Admin role required |
| INCONSISTENT | ACTIVE | Data corrected | Manual/Auto | All integrity checks pass |

### §4.4 Entry Actions

| State | Entry Action |
|---|---|
| DRAFT | Auto-generate `contract_number`. Set `created_by`, `created_at`. |
| ACTIVE | Notify stakeholders. Start renewal window monitoring. Emit `contract.activated`. |
| EXPIRED | Notify contract owner. Stop renewal monitoring. Emit `contract.expired`. |
| TERMINATED | Set `terminated_at`. Notify stakeholders. Cancel active Renewals. Emit `contract.terminated`. |
| INCONSISTENT | Notify contract owner and S10. Flag in admin dashboard. Emit `contract.inconsistent`. |
| ARCHIVED | Move documents to cold storage. Emit `contract.archived`. |
| DELETED | Hard delete record. Irreversible. |

### §4.5 Exit Actions

| State | Exit Action |
|---|---|
| ACTIVE | Clear integrity warnings. Cancel pending timers. |
| INCONSISTENT | Clear inconsistency flag. |

## Article VII: Error States

### §7.1 INCONSISTENT State

A Contract enters the INCONSISTENT state when a data integrity check fails. This can happen:
- On every update: if post-update validation fails (total ≠ sum of line items, end_date before start_date, etc.)
- On daily scheduled job: if any active contract fails integrity checks

### §7.2 Recovery

| Path | Action | Responsible | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| INCONSISTENT → ACTIVE | Correct data via edit form | Contract owner / S2 | 48 hours |
| INCONSISTENT → TERMINATED | Admin force-terminate | S9/S10 | — |

### §7.3 Forbidden States

| State | Why Forbidden |
|---|---|
| Contract without line items in ACTIVE | Would break financial tracking and renewal calculation |
| EXPIRED → DRAFT | Would allow expired contracts to bypass renewal process |
| ACTIVE → null vendor_id | Would orphan the contract from its business relationship |

## Article VIII: Archival & Deletion

### §8.1 Archival

A Contract is automatically archived when:
- Status = EXPIRED or TERMINATED
- AND `end_date + retention_period < today`
- Default retention period: 7 years (configurable per tenant)

### §8.2 Deletion

Hard deletion is only permitted for DRAFT contracts that were never activated. All other statuses use soft delete (`deleted_at` timestamp). Hard-deleted records are removed from all queries but the `contract_number` remains reserved.

### §8.3 Data Retention

| Entity | Retention | Action After |
|---|---|---|
| Contract (ACTIVE) | Indefinite | — |
| Contract (EXPIRED/TERMINATED) | 7 years from end_date | Archive → cold storage |
| Contract (ARCHIVED) | 10 years from archive_date | Purge (with legal hold check) |
| Contract (DRAFT, never activated) | 1 year from last update | Hard delete |

## Article IX: Amendments

### §9.1 What Constitutes an Amendment

Any change to an ACTIVE contract that modifies:
- Line items (add, remove, change quantity or rate)
- Clauses (add, modify)
- End date (extension or reduction)
- Total contract value

### §9.2 Amendment Recording

Every amendment creates:
1. A new `ContractVersionHistory` entry with the full snapshot
2. A timeline entry in the global activity log
3. A domain event for policy processing

### §9.3 Amendment Approval

Amendments that change total value by more than the configured threshold (default: 20%) require re-approval via the Renewal or Approval workflow. Amendments below the threshold are auto-approved.

## Article X: Migration & Compatibility

### §10.1 Schema Evolution

- New fields must be nullable or have defaults
- Existing API responses must include new fields (consumers ignore unknown fields)
- Deprecated fields are marked with a deprecation header for 6 months before removal

### §10.2 Version History

- Every state change creates a `ContractVersionHistory` entry
- The entry stores a full JSON snapshot of the contract at that point
- Snapshots are immutable
- Version history is used for audit, rollback planning, and debugging

### §10.3 Backward Compatibility

Current integer PKs are preserved for Sprint 1. UUID migration is deferred to a later sprint. All new code references contracts by their existing integer IDs. When UUID migration occurs, a mapping table will bridge old and new IDs.

---

## Article XI: Enforcement

Violation of any article in this constitution must result in:
1. **Rejection** of the violating operation with a clear error message
2. **Logging** of the violation attempt to the activity log
3. **Notification** to the system administrator if the violation is automated

No code may be merged that violates this constitution. No feature may ship that contradicts these laws.
